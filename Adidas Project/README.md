## 🧭 Project Overview

A one-page Tableau sales dashboard built from the Adidas 2021 sales dataset to turn cleaned transaction data into a concise management view of sales performance.

This project follows a practical analytics workflow: **understand the business question → prepare the data → analyze the relevant dimensions → communicate the findings through a decision-oriented reporting experience**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A raw sales dataset contains useful information but does not immediately provide management with a clear view of sales trends, product performance, geography, and overall business movement.

The core challenge is not simply producing charts or queries. It is creating a reliable analytical layer that helps a user move from **what happened** to **where it happened** and then to **what deserves further investigation**.

---

## 💡 Objective

Prepare the Adidas sales data and build a professional one-page Tableau dashboard that communicates important sales indicators without overcrowding the page.

The solution is designed around usability as well as analysis: information should be structured clearly enough that a business user can explore the relevant dimensions without going back to raw records.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset & Data Scope

Adidas sales data covering 2021, including transaction dates, sales values, quantities, products, retailers, and geographic dimensions.

> **Data note:** The README describes the dataset and analytical scope documented in the portfolio case study. It does not invent KPI values, business results, or conclusions that are not documented in the project.

---

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Analytical Workflow

The source data was cleaned and formatted before being brought into Tableau. Additional analytical fields were created during preparation to support time-based analysis and dashboard calculations.


## 🔎 Key Analytical Areas

The dashboard is designed to compare sales performance over time, identify stronger and weaker product or market areas, and provide a concise view of measures needed to understand overall sales performance.

Rather than treating the dashboard as a collection of isolated visuals, the project is structured so users can move between **summary-level performance and the underlying dimensions that may explain it**.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations / Usage

Monitor sales movement over time, compare product and market performance, and investigate the drivers behind noticeable changes rather than relying on a single headline KPI.

These are intended as analytical usage recommendations based on the documented project scope—not claims that a specific business action was actually taken.

---

## 📈 Business Impact

The project demonstrates how a raw sales dataset can be transformed into a focused, interactive Tableau reporting experience with an emphasis on clarity and decision support.

The main value of the project is the transformation from **raw or fragmented information → structured analysis → clearer business communication**.
