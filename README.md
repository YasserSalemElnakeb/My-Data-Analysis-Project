## 👋 About This Repository

Welcome to my **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence portfolio**.

This repository contains a collection of practical projects covering different stages of the data analytics lifecycle — from **data extraction and cleaning** to **analysis, visualization, dashboard development, and business storytelling**.

The goal behind these projects is not simply to create charts or write queries, but to build analytical solutions that answer real business questions and make complex information easier to understand.

# 📌 What These Projects Demonstrate

Across these projects, the main focus is on building the complete analytical workflow rather than working with visualization alone.

### 🧹 Data Preparation

Cleaning, transforming, structuring, and preparing data for analysis.

### 🗄️ Data Analysis

Using SQL, Python, Excel, DAX, and analytical techniques to investigate patterns and business questions.

### 📊 Data Visualization

Creating dashboards and reports that communicate information clearly through interactive visuals.

### 💡 Business Intelligence

Connecting analytical results with practical business questions and decision-support requirements.

### 📖 Data Storytelling

Turning complex datasets into understandable analytical stories that help users explore **what happened, where it happened, and what deserves further investigation**.
